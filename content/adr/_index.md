An architecture decision record (ADR) captures one decision: the context that forced it, the options considered, the choice and its consequences. These records explain why kael looks the way it does. Read them after the chapters they affect, or before you propose a change that touches the same trade-off.

New decisions in the library get a record in the [library repository](https://github.com/JetBerri/kael.cpp/tree/main/docs/adr) first. This section explains each record in more depth.
